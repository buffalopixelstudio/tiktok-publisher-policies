## 1. What the tool does

Buffalo Pixel Publisher is a tool used by the Buffalo Pixel team to prepare and transfer videos selected by an authorized account owner to social platforms. When TikTok integration is enabled, the tool requests the TikTok permissions shown during authorization and sends only the video and post details the user selects. Depending on the TikTok permissions and app approval status, a transfer may create a TikTok draft for the creator to review, or may submit a post through TikTok's Direct Post feature.
