Buffalo Pixel Publisher

Buffalo Pixel Publisher is a local publishing tool used by the Buffalo Pixel team to prepare and publish videos to connected social accounts. The tool runs on the operator's computer; selected videos and post details are sent to a platform only when a publishing job is started.

Availability: This tool is currently used for Buffalo Pixel's own publishing workflow. It is not offered as a public service to other creators.
